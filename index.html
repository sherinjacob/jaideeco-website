Our vision

Some of the world's best-made products come out of Thailand. Very few Thai makers get the global brand they deserve.

We close that gap. Our partners do what they do best, making excellent products. We build the brand, the story and the sales channels that put those products in front of customers worldwide.

Made in Thailand

Every product starts with a Thai maker: the herbal traditions, pet food expertise, Muay Thai craft and silversmithing the country is known for.

Built for global channels

We sell where customers already shop: Amazon in the US and UK, our own online stores, and the new AI-driven shopping assistants.

Our partners' story, told well

We put the people and craft behind each product at the centre of the brand, in modern design that customers abroad understand.

For manufacturers

We're looking for long-term partners, not one-off orders

We want to work with a small number of makers in each category and grow with them. Here's how a partnership comes together.

  1. Get to know each other

    We learn about your company, team and products through calls and factory visits in Thailand.

  2. Co-develop the range

    Together we choose and refine 3–5 products, agree packaging and branding, and test samples.

  3. Launch a trial range

    We launch on Amazon US/UK and our own store, with your craft at the heart of the brand.

  4. Grow together

    As products find customers, we expand the range and the markets, with you as our core maker.

What we look for in a partner

  • Certified production (GMP, ISO, BRC, RJC or equivalent)
  • Experience exporting to the US, UK or EU
  • R&D and product development capability
  • Openness to working with a new, growing brand
  • Clear, timely communication in English or Thai
  • Pride in your craft and your people
